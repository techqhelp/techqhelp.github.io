Tips to Sell Agricultural Products at the Best Price

  2. Write detailed descriptions — Mention variety, weight, quantity, quality grade, and if the product is organic or certified.
  3. Set competitive prices — Check mandi rates on MYHAL and price your products fairly. Buyers compare multiple listings.
  4. Respond quickly — Reply to buyer enquiries within an hour. Fast responses lead to faster deals.
  5. Be honest about quality — Building trust with buyers leads to repeat orders and referrals.
  6. Post in your local language — MYHAL supports 13+ languages. Posting in your local language attracts more buyers in your area.
  7. Update listings regularly — Remove sold items and update prices based on market rates to keep your profile active.
  8. Sell seasonal produce early — List your seasonal crops before the peak season to attract early buyers at premium prices.

Where Can Farmers Sell Agricultural Products in India?

There are several ways farmers can sell their produce in India:

1. Online Agriculture Marketplaces (Best Option)

MYHAL is India's #1 agriculture marketplace app where farmers can sell crops, vegetables, fruits, dairy, livestock, and farm equipment for free. With 525+ categories and 13+ language support, it's designed specifically for Indian farmers. No commission, no middlemen — just direct buyer-seller connections.

2. Government Mandis (APMC Markets)

Traditional government-regulated wholesale markets where farmers bring their produce. While they provide a formal market, farmers often face price manipulation by middlemen and have to pay commission fees. Travel and transportation costs add up.

3. eNAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market)

A government initiative to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities. While it aims to provide better prices, it requires registration and the process can be complex for many farmers.

4. Direct Farm-to-Consumer

Selling directly at farmer's markets or through apps like MYHAL. This approach gives you the highest margins since there are no middlemen. MYHAL makes this easy by connecting you with local buyers through your phone.

5. Contract Farming

Pre-agreed sales with companies or retailers. This guarantees a price but limits your flexibility. Best suited for large-scale producers.

Selling Specific Products — Detailed Guides

How to Sell Vegetables and Fruits Online

Fresh vegetables and fruits are the most searched products on MYHAL. To sell effectively:

  • List your produce with clear photos showing freshness and quality
  • Mention the variety (e.g., Alphonso mango, Shimla apple, Desi tomato)
  • Specify quantity available (per kg, per crate, per quintal)
  • Update availability daily since fruits and vegetables are perishable
  • Popular items: Tomato, potato, onion, cauliflower, cabbage, mango, banana, apple, guava, papaya, orange, watermelon, pomegranate

How to Sell Grains and Crops Online

Grains like wheat, rice, maize, bajra and jowar are staple commodities with huge demand:

  • Mention the grain variety and quality grade
  • Specify moisture content and whether it's sun-dried or machine-dried
  • Price per quintal or per kg
  • Mention if it's organic or conventionally grown
  • Compare with current mandi rates before pricing

How to Sell Dairy Products Online

Dairy is a high-demand category. You can sell:

  • Milk — Cow milk, buffalo milk, A2 milk (mention fat content and quantity)
  • Ghee — Desi ghee, A2 ghee (mention source and preparation method)
  • Paneer, curd, butter — Fresh homemade dairy products
  • Highlight if your products are organic, homemade, or from specific breeds

How to Sell Livestock and Animals Online

MYHAL has dedicated categories for livestock trading:

  • Cows — HF cow, Jersey cow, Rathi cow, Sahiwal (mention breed, age, milk yield)
  • Buffaloes — Murrah buffalo (mention lactation, milk per day)
  • Goats — Sirohi, Beetal, Barbari, Jamunapari (mention breed, weight)
  • Sheep, poultry, dogs — All available on MYHAL
  • Upload multiple photos from different angles and mention health/vaccination details

How to Sell Farm Equipment Online

Both new and used farm equipment sell well on MYHAL:

  • Tractors, harvesters, rotavators, sprayers, tillers, trailers
  • Mention brand, model, HP rating, hours used, and condition
