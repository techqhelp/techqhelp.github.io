Tips for Buying Agricultural Products Online

  1. Compare multiple sellers — Don't buy from the first listing. Check prices and quality from 3-4 sellers for the best deal.
  2. Check photos carefully — Look at product photos to assess freshness, ripeness, and quality before contacting the seller.
  3. Ask about growing practices — If you want organic or pesticide-free products, ask the farmer directly about their methods.
  4. Buy seasonal — Seasonal produce is cheaper, fresher, and more nutritious. Check what's in season in your region.
  5. Negotiate for bulk — Buying larger quantities? Most farmers offer better per-kg rates for bulk orders.
  6. Clarify delivery/pickup — Discuss who handles transport, delivery charges, and packaging before confirming the order.
  7. Build long-term relationships — Once you find a good farmer, maintain the relationship for consistent quality and priority supply.
  8. Check units carefully — Prices may be per kg, per quintal, per piece, or per crate. Clarify the unit before comparing.
