MYHAL is India's easiest agriculture classified app. Whether you want to buy farm produce, sell crops, rent a tractor, or find fertilizers — here's how it works.

Step 1: Download & Register

Download the MYHAL app from Google Play Store. Sign up free with your phone number. Available in 13+ Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati and more.

Step 2: Post a Free Ad or Browse

List your farm products — tractors, seeds, fertilizers, livestock, crops, organic manure — with photos and price. Or browse thousands of agriculture classifieds near you.

Step 3: Connect & Close Deals

Chat directly with verified buyers and sellers. No middlemen, no commission. Rent tractors and harvesters from nearby owners. Get fair prices and quick payments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MYHAL?

MYHAL is India's trusted agriculture classifieds and rental marketplace. You can buy, sell or rent farm products like tractors, seeds, fertilizers, livestock, crops, and farm machinery — all in one free app. It connects farmers directly with buyers without middlemen.

Is it free to post ads on MYHAL?

Yes, posting ads on MYHAL is completely free. Farmers can list their agriculture products, upload photos, set prices, and connect with genuine buyers at no cost. There are no commissions or hidden charges.

Can I rent a tractor on MYHAL?

Yes. MYHAL has a dedicated rental section where you can find tractors, harvesters, rotavators, and other farm equipment for rent from nearby owners. Popular brands like Mahindra 575, Swaraj 744, John Deere 5050 and New Holland 3630 are listed with hourly rates.

What can I buy and sell on MYHAL?

You can buy and sell a wide range of agriculture products including wheat, rice, cotton, vegetables, fruits, dairy products, livestock (cows, buffaloes, goats), farm machinery, tractors, seeds, fertilizers, organic manure, vermi compost, landscaping plants, and ornamental plants.

How many languages does MYHAL support?

MYHAL supports 13+ Indian languages including Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia, Kannada, Marathi, Assamese, and Urdu. You can post ads and browse listings in your preferred local language.

Is MYHAL available in my area?

MYHAL is available across all Indian states. The app uses your location to show nearby listings. Farmers from Delhi, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, UP, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and all other states are using MYHAL to trade agriculture products locally.
